ABOUT ME

Teaching, research and real-world technology.

I am Dr. Manzoor Ansari, an Assistant Professor in Computer Science. My academic and research work focuses on intelligent computing systems that connect data, devices and cloud platforms to solve real-world problems.

My research interests include IoT-cloud integration, artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, time-series forecasting, environmental monitoring and data analytics.
